Three assessments track the semester's progression from observation to
defence. The Queue Autopsy asks you to observe and analyse a real waiting
system; the Policy Trial asks you to compare ordering rules on a declared
dataset and defend one; The Honest Queue asks for a full, defended redesign
of the SlopU Office of Everything. Each builds directly on the one before it.
