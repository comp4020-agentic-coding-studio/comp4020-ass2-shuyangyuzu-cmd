The course moves through four arguments, three weeks each: measuring a queue
honestly (weeks 1–3), choosing and defending an ordering rule (weeks 4–6),
deciding what a system can honestly promise the person waiting inside it
(weeks 7–9), and defending a full redesign under pressure (weeks 10–12). Each
lecture below builds on the one before it and feeds that same week's Queue
Lab.
