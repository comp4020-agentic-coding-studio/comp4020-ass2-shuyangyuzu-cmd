joining the teaching team, which is where most of the course's synthetic
incident reports quietly come from. He teaches the even-numbered weeks —
choosing an ordering rule, reading a stuck queue, auditing the Priority
Lane — and marks the Queue Autopsy and Policy Trial.

He still flinches slightly whenever a ticket dispenser makes a grinding
noise.
