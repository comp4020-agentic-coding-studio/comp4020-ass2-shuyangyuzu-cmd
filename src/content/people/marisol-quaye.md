Marisol Quaye designed SLOP2516 around a single question: what does a queue
actually measure, once you stop assuming it measures fairness by default.
She teaches the odd-numbered weeks — measurement, communication, and the
final defence — and maintains the Office of Everything's case file between
semesters, including whichever new memo, complaint or incident report each
cohort's redesign is tested against.

Before convening this course, she spent several years arguing with the
Office of Everything's own front counter about what its "estimated wait"
sign actually meant. The sign has since been retired.
