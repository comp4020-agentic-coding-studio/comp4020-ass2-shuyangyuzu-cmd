Renata Voss designed SLOP2516 around a single question: what a queue
actually measures, once you stop assuming it measures fairness by default.
She teaches the odd-numbered weeks — measurement, honest communication, and
the final defence — and maintains the Office of Everything's case file
between semesters, adding whichever new memo, complaint or incident report
each cohort's redesign is tested against.

She keeps a running list of every "estimated wait" sign she has personally
found unconvincing. The Office of Everything's is on it.
