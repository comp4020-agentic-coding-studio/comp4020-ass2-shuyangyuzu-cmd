# Process overview

I wanted a course that attracts attention through a recognisable irritation,
then rewards sustained study. In SLOP2516, *Your Call Is Important to Us*, the
irritation is waiting at a university service desk. My design position was
that a good course should revisit one consequential question with increasingly
powerful tools, rather than accumulate twelve loosely related topics. The
Office of Everything gives that progression a single fictional institution;
Measure, Choose, Communicate and Defend each demand a different decision
([`82a9751`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/82a9751)).

I translated that position into constraints before accepting a full site.
`CLAUDE.md` requires one claim across twelve weeks, a new decision each week,
and a visible boundary between synthetic case material and real-world claims.
Deadpan institutional language lets the satire emerge from what the Office
considers reasonable. I preferred this to adding jokes to otherwise generic
teaching material. The structure tests protect matched lecture/lab weeks,
assessment weights and the course identity; I deliberately did not ask a test
to decide whether the course is compelling. That remains a reading judgment
([`82a9751`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/82a9751),
[`96ecbcc`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/96ecbcc)).

The strongest correction concerned what an exercise could legitimately ask.
Week 10 initially invited conclusions that its released data could not support.
The revision separated category shares from an aggregate wait-time association
and explicitly named missing evidence. Regression checks preserve those
boundaries, although matching phrases cannot prove every future sentence is
sound. The later Week 9 correction applies the same principle: unrecorded
departure times require hypothetical sensitivity calculations, not a confident
claim that the overall average must be higher. These changes made evidential
restraint part of the learning task itself
([`15a8c54`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/15a8c54),
[`12895fd`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/12895fd)).

The Policy Lab makes this restraint usable. Fixed tickets let learners inspect
who pays for an improved average without treating a policy as universally best.
Its scope was declared before the site implementation; tests compare that
implementation with separately calculated reference results and check arrival,
non-preemption and closure rules. Agreement increases confidence in the
calculation, not in the realism of its assumptions. Linking the Lab to weekly
work and assessments makes the experiment preparation for a defended choice,
not an isolated website attraction
([`35a91ae`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/35a91ae),
[`8434d4d`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/8434d4d),
[`12895fd`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/12895fd)).

I also needed the entrance to explain the course before asking visitors to
read it. I directed the Service Hall revision around a fictional receipt,
four teaching windows and a concrete policy comparison. The homepage reuses
the site's simulator rather than copying numbers from conversation. The narrow
four-window layout exposed a limit of the previous green build: a prose-width
rule could still damage the reading experience. I asked for real desktop and
mobile inspection, clear entry links and unbroken stage titles, rather than
accepting compilation as visual evidence
([`c0cc15f`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/c0cc15f)).

The final review made alignment more concrete. Codex read CMU's guidance
on objectives, practice and assessment, alongside the *Calling Bullshit*
syllabus; this was a late review, not the origin of my earlier design.
The comparison exposed a mistake green tests missed: a 26-minute wait had
become a 26-minute service in the next week. The revision separates sample
omission from model intervention and accepts a longest observed wait even
when no dramatic outlier occurs. The harness now asks whether each assessed
action was rehearsed and whether quantities retain their meaning. This
protects the course's commitment to evidence without pretending a keyword
test can judge teaching quality ([`4842c8e`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-shuyangyuzu-cmd/commit/4842c8e)). Numerical checks and
agent review support that decision; student learning has not been tested.
